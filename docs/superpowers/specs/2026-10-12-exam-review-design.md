# Unit exam and spaced error review

The personal workbook follows a book, one official unit per weekday and weekly review on both weekend days. User approved: all unit exercises unlock the exam; passing it closes the selected unit and day. Existing closed days/ranks remain valid.

Builder choices: 13 distinct existing examples, six or seven automatically checked formats (two choice, two fill, up to two order, listening fill, dictation, three corrections, two transformations). No new generated questions. Pass at 80% based on whole correct answers, never rounded percentage. No hints, correctness, translations or manual override during the exam; all answers are reviewed at the end. Per-answer draft is saved; a finished attempt is counted once. Retakes are unlimited and grant no extra course day or XP. Passing another unit/weekend saves its assessment but cannot close an extra day. A passed unit practiced earlier can be officially claimed when selected on a weekday.

Errors become a persistent journal. Wrong responses reset reinforcement stage and record last answer, format, recurrence count and next due date. Three correct checks at due dates advance through 1, 3 and 7 day intervals; after the last check the item is mastered. Correct answers before the due date give practice, without advancing. The same checked event/manual override is idempotent; successful overrides undo that event instead of adding reinforcement. Legacy errors migrate to stage zero, due today. Mastered records remain visible separately and relapse returns them to the queue.

Review starts with due items, ordered by date and recurrence, up to 20. Gap questions alternate form, order and dictation where available; corrections/transformations retain their meaningful formats. Show why, translation, previous answer, stage and next date after checking. Optional early practice does not skip the interval. Weekend review prioritizes due errors in this week's units while retaining coverage of every studied unit.

Vanilla JS, no dependency/build changes. Add focused pure modules for assessment and review state, keep rendering in app.js. Generic export/import includes new keys. Add regression tests for threshold boundaries, mixed unique bank, resumption, repeat submission, migration, relapse, spacing, malformed imports and daily closure. Browser QA uses an isolated localhost origin so personal progress stays intact.
