# Weekly error revision and admission to new lessons

The learner wants weekend revision based on their own mistakes, explanations and additional practice. From the following Monday, unfinished work on the previous week's mistakes must prevent starting new lessons until the work is completed.

The weekly assignment groups rejected answers by source example and calendar week. Each example has its saved answer, correct answer, Russian translation and explanation. Completion requires reading the explanation, correctly answering the original task and correctly completing two additional examples of that unit. Every step saves immediately. Existing spaced repetition remains independent; its future due dates do not delay the weekly assignment.

Week boundaries follow local device dates, Monday through Sunday. Warnings appear in the calendar and contents before the deadline. Every unfinished previous week remains a debt, including after the next Monday; the oldest debt is presented first. A week without mistakes does not block anything. The current week's errors do not block that week's lessons. New rejected answers to the same source reopen that week's work; later ordinary correct practice does not erase the assignment. Explicit cancellation of a false rejection cancels its matching weekly occurrence and restores prior work when present.

Blocking applies to unclosed unit routes (including direct exercise/exam/context links), mixed practice that includes unclosed units, and closing a new daily unit. Revision routes and closed units remain available. The block disappears immediately after completing the outstanding assignment. No old grade, course claim, book mark or XP is removed.

On first installation, seed only errors with a known rejection date in the installation week. Future weeks capture failures when they happen. Undated legacy errors retain their existing spaced review and do not create guessed historical debts. The new weekly state is included in the normal progress export.

Additional examples use the existing reviewed unit recipes with two new vocabulary contexts, separate from assessment banks A/B and contextual stories. They are labelled practice of the unit's theme, not a claimed diagnosis of the precise cause. Listening errors receive listening practice. No automatic network request or paid generation is needed.

Use the existing workbook styling and persistent-state conventions. Implement and review in the current session without subagents, preserving the user's working copy and live progress.
